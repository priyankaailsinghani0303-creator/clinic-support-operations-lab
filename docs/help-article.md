# Help Article: Appointment Not Appearing in Calendar

**Category:** Booking & Calendar  
**Audience:** Clinic owners and front-desk staff  
**Status:** Portfolio demonstration — generic troubleshooting guidance

## Problem

An appointment was booked online, but it is not appearing in your clinic calendar.

Follow these steps to check whether the appointment is hidden by calendar settings or whether further investigation is needed.

## Step 1 — Check the Appointment Date

Make sure your calendar is showing the correct date.

If your clinic uses multiple time zones, confirm that the appointment time matches your clinic's configured time zone.

## Step 2 — Check Calendar Filters

If your calendar supports filters, check whether the correct provider, location, and appointment status are selected.

Try displaying all relevant appointments to see whether the booking appears.

## Step 3 — Verify the Booking Status

If your system has a booking history or appointment list, check whether the appointment is marked as:

- Confirmed
- Pending
- Cancelled

**Important:** Do not create a duplicate booking until you have checked whether the original appointment exists.

## Step 4 — Refresh the Calendar

Refresh the calendar view and check again.

If the appointment is still missing, note whether the problem affects one appointment or multiple bookings.

## Step 5 — Contact Support

If the issue continues, contact your support team with:

- Appointment date and approximate time
- Selected provider or service
- Booking confirmation status, if available
- Any error message
- A screenshot with patient information hidden

Please do not send passwords or sensitive patient records through unapproved support channels.

## What Happens Next?

The support team can review the reported booking status, calendar settings, and troubleshooting results.

If the issue cannot be resolved, further technical investigation may be required.
